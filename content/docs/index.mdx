Find a passage you remember, compare papers, or recover your own notes. Start with a paper you know so you can check what the assistant retrieves.

## 1. Choose your client and connection

<ConnectionOptions />

[Shared group library? Start with the review-team guide.](/docs/group-libraries-for-review-teams) ChatGPT requires a public HTTPS connector; a free local `npx` process cannot connect to ChatGPT. Self-hosting that remote is free software, but infrastructure and any API usage are your responsibility.

## 2. Check what is ready

Before a large research question, ask which library is connected, whether one PDF is readable, and whether search uses keywords or vectors. [Search readiness](/docs/search-readiness) explains metadata, notes, PDF coverage, embedding setup, and index limits. [Hosted plans](/pricing#plans) start at €7/month; the [hosted requirements](/pricing#before-you-pay) are worth checking before subscribing.

## 3. Get your first verified passage

Follow [your first research task](/docs/first-research-task): find a known paper, retrieve a quotation, and open the page in Zotero to check it. You do not need to finish a whole-library semantic index first.

Then try [annotations to literature notes](/docs/annotations-to-literature-notes), or [compare evidence from several papers](/docs/first-research-task#compare-evidence). AI conclusions can still be wrong even when references exist.
