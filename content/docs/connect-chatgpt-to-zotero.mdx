Zoteus is an open-source MCP server that gives ChatGPT access to your Zotero library. Once it is connected you can ask ChatGPT to find the paper you saved three years ago, read a page range from its PDF, build a bibliography in the style your journal wants, or tag the items you are screening. References come from your library, but you still need to verify each passage and the conclusions drawn from it.

ChatGPT connects to MCP servers over HTTPS only, from OpenAI's cloud. It cannot start the local `npx @oscardvs/zoteus` server that Claude Desktop, Claude Code and Cursor use, and it cannot reach the Zotero app on your computer. That leaves two routes, and both reach your library through your Zotero account online.

| Route | What to do | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| The hosted connector, listed in ChatGPT's plugin directory | [Install the plugin, start the trial, sign in](#add-zoteus-to-chatgpt) | [14 days free, then from €7/month](/pricing#plans) |
| Your own server | [Run the OAuth remote on a public HTTPS host](/docs/remote-oauth), then [add it as a custom MCP server](#your-own-server) | Free software; your own hosting costs |

## Before you start

- **A ChatGPT account that can install plugins.** The plugin directory is open on every ChatGPT plan, under **Plugins** in the sidebar. OpenAI says whether an individual plugin can be installed depends on your plan, workspace, role and region, and on Business, Enterprise and Edu an administrator can turn plugins off. The hosted connector needs no Developer mode.
- **A Zotero account with your library synced.** ChatGPT reaches your library through zotero.org, so anything you have not synced to Zotero's servers is invisible to it. PDFs in Zotero's cloud storage can be read; PDFs that live only on your own disk stay out of reach. Check sync in the Zotero desktop app under **Settings**, then **Sync**.
- **A Zoteus subscription key, or your own remote.** Subscribers get the key by email after checkout on the [pricing page](/pricing#plans). If you self-host, you need the public HTTPS URL of your instance, which means running a server on the public internet; [Remote OAuth](/docs/remote-oauth) covers that.

<Callout title="Before subscribing">
Check [hosted requirements and limits](/pricing#before-you-pay): client access, synced PDF availability, which libraries you want indexed, and the provider/coverage your research needs. Metadata sync alone does not make local-only or WebDAV file bytes readable. Zoteus does not include a separate AI subscription.
</Callout>

## Add Zoteus to ChatGPT [#add-zoteus-to-chatgpt]

<PricingCta source="connect-chatgpt" />

1. **Subscribe, or start later.** You can start the 14-day free trial on the [pricing page](/pricing#plans) now, or from the Zoteus sign-in page in step 4. Checkout is handled by Polar and the subscription key arrives by email. Self-hosters skip this step.
2. **Find Zoteus.** Open the [Zoteus plugin page in ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6adcac3c4d8c81918b04f04ec0640974), or click **Plugins** in ChatGPT's left sidebar and search for **Zoteus**.
3. **Install it.** Click **Install plugin**, then **Connect Zoteus**.
4. **Sign in.** A new window opens the Zoteus page **Connect ChatGPT to your Zotero library**. If you have no key yet, click **Start your free trial**: checkout opens in a new tab, and you paste the key from Polar's email back into this page, which stays open for an hour. With a key, paste it under **Already subscribed?**. The page then goes to zotero.org, where you sign in and approve the permissions Zoteus asks for; untick write access there if you want a read-only connection. The popup returns to ChatGPT.
5. **Use it.** ChatGPT uses Zoteus when your request is about your library. To call it directly, type **@Zoteus** in the composer, or click **+** and pick **Zoteus**. Start with *Am I connected to my Zotero library?*

If you added Zoteus by hand before it was listed, install the listed plugin as above; you can then remove the hand-added one so ChatGPT does not offer two.

## Your own server [#your-own-server]

A self-hosted Zoteus is not in the plugin directory, so you add it to ChatGPT as a custom MCP server. OpenAI currently offers custom MCP servers on the Business, Enterprise and Edu plans, in ChatGPT on the web, where an administrator may have to grant the **Create plugins with MCPs** permission; Pro accounts can connect them with read-only tools. Developer mode is no longer part of this.

1. **Add the server.** Open **Plugins**, click the **+** button, then **Add custom MCP server**.
2. **Fill it in.** Name it `Zoteus`. Under **Connection**, enter `https://<your-host>/mcp`, and choose OAuth for authentication: ChatGPT discovers the OAuth endpoints and registers itself with Zoteus.
3. **Create it.** Tick **I understand and want to continue**, click **Create as a plugin**, and review the tools ChatGPT found.
4. **Install and sign in.** Install the new plugin and connect it. The sign-in window opens your instance's consent page; [Remote OAuth](/docs/remote-oauth) covers what it asks.
5. **Use it.** In a chat, type **@** and pick it.

## First successful research task

[Find a known paper, retrieve a passage and verify its page](/docs/first-research-task). Check [search readiness](/docs/search-readiness) before interpreting a missing semantic hit as missing evidence.

## What to ask next

1. *Search my library for papers about urban heat islands.*
2. *Which papers in my library argue against open-plan offices? Quote the passage and give me the page.*
3. *Format a bibliography of the five most relevant papers in APA style.*
4. *Give me the outline of the PDF attached to that paper, with page numbers, then read me pages 4 to 6.*
5. *Tag the three most recent of those with `to-read`.*

The second question can use search by meaning to locate candidates, followed by passage retrieval. The first request can start a background index build. Semantic ranking needs active embeddings; PDF-body indexing is opt-in and may cover only part of a library. Check [search readiness](/docs/search-readiness) first. Direct keyword lookup works without that index.

## What ChatGPT asks before a write

ChatGPT controls tool confirmations through the app permissions. Behaviour can vary by account, tool and saved choices. Review the current permissions instead of assuming every write will ask first.

That behaviour is the plugin's **Permissions** setting, on its page under **Plugins**. The options are **Always ask**, **Allow read actions**, **Allow low-risk actions** (the default) and **Allow all actions**. To review writes, select the setting that asks before writes (for example **Allow read actions**) and ask for a proposed change first. Zotero authorization must also permit the write.

## Do you need a Zotero API key?

No. ChatGPT signs you in at zotero.org through the plugin, and you never create or paste a key. On the zotero.org approval page you choose what the connection may do: leave write access ticked if you want ChatGPT to add tags, file items or edit records, or untick it for a read-only connection. You can revoke the access at any time from [zotero.org/settings/keys](https://www.zotero.org/settings/keys).

## When something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Zoteus does not show up under **Plugins**, or cannot be installed | Open the [Zoteus plugin page](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6adcac3c4d8c81918b04f04ec0640974) directly. OpenAI ties plugin availability to your ChatGPT plan, workspace and region; on Business, Enterprise and Edu, ask an administrator whether plugins are allowed. |
| **Add custom MCP server** is missing (your own server) | Your plan or workspace does not allow custom MCP servers. OpenAI offers them on Business, Enterprise and Edu, on the web; on Business and Enterprise an administrator grants **Create plugins with MCPs**. See [OpenAI's article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12584461-connecting-custom-mcp-servers-in-chatgpt). |
| The sign-in popup closes without a Zotero page | The popup was blocked. Allow popups for chatgpt.com and click the button again. |
| ChatGPT says it cannot reach the server | The URL must be a public HTTPS address ending in `/mcp`. A self-hosted instance on `localhost`, behind a firewall, or on plain HTTP cannot be reached from OpenAI's cloud; see [Remote OAuth](/docs/remote-oauth). |
| ChatGPT asks permission on every write | The plugin's **Permissions** setting is on **Always ask** or **Allow read actions**. This can be intentional. Keep confirmations if you want to review changes; inspect the proposed edit and approve only the action you intend. |
| ChatGPT cannot find something you know is in Zotero | Check account, selected library and permissions, then sync. Try exact title lookup. Indexed search may be capped, partial or keyword-only; a PDF may lack accessible bytes or a text layer. See [search readiness](/docs/search-readiness) and [missing PDFs](/docs/missing-pdfs). |
| Something else | [Open an issue](https://github.com/oscardvs/zoteus/issues) with what you tried and what you saw, or email [support@zoteus.com](mailto:support@zoteus.com) about the hosted connector. Never paste your subscription key. |

## Frequently asked questions [#faq]

### Can ChatGPT read the PDFs in my Zotero library?

Yes, through Zoteus, for PDFs stored in Zotero's cloud storage. Zoteus reaches your library through your Zotero account online, so ChatGPT can read those PDFs, quote passages with their page numbers and look at a page as an image. PDFs that exist only on your own disk or in WebDAV storage are out of reach for ChatGPT; see [missing PDFs](/docs/missing-pdfs).

### Is Zoteus only for editing my library, or can it read my PDFs?

Zoteus reads first. In ChatGPT it searches your Zotero library by keyword or, where embeddings are active, by meaning, returns passages from your PDFs with page numbers, finds your own notes and PDF annotations, and can compare papers in a cited evidence table. It can also add items by DOI or arXiv id, tag and file items and edit records, but only if you leave write access ticked when you approve Zoteus on zotero.org.

### Do I need to install anything?

Nothing on your computer. Zoteus is a plugin in ChatGPT's plugin directory: you install it under **Plugins** in ChatGPT, sign in with your Zotero account, and there is nothing to download or update. You need a Zotero library synced to zotero.org, and OpenAI says plugin availability depends on your ChatGPT plan, workspace and region.

### Is there a free version of Zoteus for ChatGPT?

Zoteus is MIT-licensed and free to self-host with every feature, but ChatGPT only connects to servers on the public internet, so the free route for ChatGPT means running the Zoteus OAuth remote on your own HTTPS host at your own hosting cost and adding it as a custom MCP server, which OpenAI offers on the Business, Enterprise and Edu plans. The free local install works in Claude Desktop, Claude Code and Cursor, not in ChatGPT. The hosted Zoteus connector costs €69 a year or €7 a month for one researcher, or €99 a month for a lab of up to ten, and does not include a ChatGPT subscription.

### Does Zoteus work with Zotero group libraries in ChatGPT?

Yes. Zoteus lets ChatGPT use the group libraries your Zotero account belongs to, within what your Zotero group role allows: ask ChatGPT which groups it can see, then name the group in your request. Each library, personal or group, can have its own search index, and ChatGPT can search several of them at once. The hosted connector currently searches by keyword, including PDF full text; search by meaning needs a server with embeddings switched on, such as a self-hosted remote; see [group libraries for review teams](/docs/group-libraries-for-review-teams).

### Can ChatGPT cite page numbers from my Zotero PDFs?

Yes. When ChatGPT asks Zoteus for a passage, Zoteus returns the text from the PDF with its page number and the nearest heading, so ChatGPT can quote it with the page. Zoteus also formats bibliographies with citeproc-js in any CSL style, such as APA, Vancouver or Chicago. Check each quoted passage against the PDF page before you rely on it.
